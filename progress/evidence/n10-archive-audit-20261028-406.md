# N10.3/#406 #404 archive reuse and gap audit

## Scope and result

Read-only audit of the #404 integrated calibration archive. No SITL was started and no flight/control code or threshold was changed. The snapshot and tracking evaluators were replayed into a separate archive directory; the acceptance replay reproduces the original overall `INSUFFICIENT` result.

This closes the offline reuse/audit subtask, not the N10 exit. The archive is enough to keep the optional 26-query capture from triggering a repeat flight, but it does not resolve raw-map classification, establish a geometry-backed global tracking threshold, or fully close resource/queue evidence.

## Map classification

- Replayed `evaluate_n10_occupancy_snapshot.py` against the #404 source archive. Runtime epoch/map/frame identity matches and source watermark coverage is `covered`: watermark `83.028 s`, snapshot last depth source `83.988 s`, difference `0.960 s`. The stored NPZ readback is exact and includes both raw occupancy and inflated arrays.
- The 0.8 × 0.8 × 2.8 m inserted box ROI contains 54 known-free voxel centers; 18 of those cells have all eight voxel corners inside the solid truth box. The draft zero-count semantic criterion therefore reproduces `FAIL`. Unknown remains distinct: 1,731 unknown and 7 raw-occupied center-inside cells; no fully-contained raw-occupied cells. Only the x-negative and y-negative faces have occupied support (20 each; maximum measured center error 0.0516 m); the other four faces are unsupported.
- The box was inserted at `79.960 s`; the snapshot source stamp is `83.988 s`. The snapshot is current at map level, but the archive has no per-voxel update timestamps or exact cloud-to-fusion association. Earlier and later center-sphere observations report unknown (0 free) at that center, while the 18 classified cells are not individually identified in the live query ledger. Stale pre-insertion free votes are plausible, but the exact cause is not established. Preserve `FAIL` pending cell-level attribution; do not lower or delete the criterion.
- The separate `/mapping/get_map_status` 26-query capture is unavailable (0 responses). This does not invalidate the complete identity-bound raw/inflated array or change its 18-cell result, so it is not by itself a reason to repeat #404. Continue root-cause work offline first.

## Tracking and body geometry

- The replay pairs 9,381 matching READY commands with truth over `83.688 sim s`, with maximum truth bracket gap `0.004 s`. Position tracking error is P95 `0.333776 m`, max `0.342208 m`.
- Sampled actual full-body clearance reaches `0.174246 m`; the independent 4 ms model-bounded airborne interval minimum is `0.162761 m`. Both exceed the draft `0.10 m` SITL candidate for this run. The dynamic box alone has a sampled margin-minus-motion lower bound of `1.212211 m`.
- A single scalar translation-only bound is negative in 434/9,381 samples (`4.63%`), with worst value `−0.158992 m`; that is not a collision finding. At the minimum actual-body-clearance sample, tracking error is `0.275979 m`, but it consumes `0.017968 m` of clearance relative to the command at that same sample. This confirms that error norm alone is a poor proxy for directional clearance. The run supports the route-specific measured clearance, not a universal scalar tracking limit. Keep the tracking acceptance threshold unresolved; do not remove it or call it passed.

The two GPU UUIDs exactly match #404 `gpu.txt`, and the archived run saw 64 CPUs. The inventory is a strong same-host match; CPU model/RAM are recorded as host capacity, not as a direct #404-time sample.
- Source audit finds no finite bound or high-water report for VINS estimator `accBuf`, `gyrBuf`, or `featureBuf` queues. The ROS IMU subscription uses KeepLast(2000); stereo subscriptions use SensorDataQoS; VINS application image queues are `std::queue` and are trimmed after synchronization, but their high-water is not recorded. Process RSS cannot substitute for queue occupancy. Resource acceptance remains `INSUFFICIENT` until queue behavior is measured under representative motion/replanning and the mapping-drop behavior is related to its existing native freshness/timeout behavior.
